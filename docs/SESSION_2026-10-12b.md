  q = q_kin (1 + (dh/dx)/S_0), implicit by construction.  Paired 240 h: corrected 16 % faster, 22 % fewer
  Newton its, max 1 cm head difference.  Full year pb4_corr: scores identical to pb4_rec_c4 (KGE within 0.01),
  25 % fewer Newton its.  Candidate default for the class deck; not switched yet.
