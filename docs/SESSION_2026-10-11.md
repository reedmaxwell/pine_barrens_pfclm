# Session record, 2026-10-11 to 12: building and calibrating the Pine Barrens domain

A checkpoint written mid-session so the state can be picked up cold.  The calibration table is in
`calibration_log.md`; this file is the narrative and the decisions.

## Why this domain

An East Coast counterpart to east_river_pfclm for two classes: CEE586 (Reed, Princeton, physical
hydrology, nine graduate students) and Laura's CUAHSI virtual course starting 2026-11-05.  East
River is snow, steep slopes, and runoff timing.  The Pine Barrens is sand, slopes of 0.002, a water
table within a metre of the surface, and streams that are baseflow: the regime where the kinematic
and diffusive waves are supposed to differ, and where the groundwater system decides the hydrograph.
A Catskills box (East Branch Delaware at Margaretville, 424 km2, a reference gage) is the planned
third domain, from the same build script with a new gage list.

## What was built

- Domain: five watersheds (North and South Branch Rancocas, Wading below the Oswego confluence,
  Toms River, Cedar Creek), 1,246 cells of the CONUS2 1 km grid in a 55 x 55 box.  Delineated on the
  CONUS2 flow-direction grid, identical by Python and by the R PriorityFlow package, cell counts equal
  to HydroData's drainage areas.  The two Wading branches are not separable at 1 km, so their sum
  is the target; Mullica near Batsto and Batsto River are dropped (network areas off by 3x).
- Boundary slopes: outward components zeroed on every hillslope boundary face, all four face types,
  because ParFlow evaluates an open west or south face from the cell's own slope
  (overlandflow_eval_Kin.c).  Eleven river-cell bank faces near the outlets are kept and their
  outflow is scored with the outlet; the spill share is 0.3 to 0.98 of outlet discharge and is a
  1 km network artifact, not a mass-balance problem.  Gates: outward faces only at the listed
  exceptions, no kinematic sinks, no pits.  Slope magnitudes never changed (Reed).
- Inputs: CONUS2.1 statics via subsettools; WY2008 CW3E forcing clipped from the Delaware bed
  (1,076 mm over the mask); CLM land cover from CONUS2 (472 grassland and 179 urban cells where
  the real Pine Barrens is forest, a known defect); USGS daily flow at seven gages and AmeriFlux
  daily ET at three towers from HydroData (latent heat halved for the hourly-SUM aggregation,
  UTC stamps shifted to EST).
- Deck: the East River deck with the CONUS2.1 subsurface table, the Delaware 2026-08-11 ET
  package (interception alpha 0.25, dewmx 0.1, cropland LAI 4.5 and vcmx25 24, urban top-layer
  permeability x0.2), variant options for geology K (optionally horizontal only), anisotropy, the
  flow barrier (off or moved), roughness, and the four overland schemes.  deck.py holds the shared
  keys.
- Spin-up (East River SPINUP_HOWTO, the Delaware notes): a coupled year, its signed P - ET as a
  top-layer source, guarded steady solves with a seepage face then a kinematic top on the
  etguard_adt build, then coupled recursion on the east-river-2026 build.  respin.sh runs it.
- Scoring: evaluate.py (outlets plus bank spill, nested Greenwood gage, tower ET, formula matched
  to the run's overland scheme), compare_runs.py, plot_et.py, recession.py, case_table.py.

## What was learned, in order

1. The Delaware run's end state has no surface water here (water table 0.9 m down in the channels);
   the CONUS2 steady state does (0.2 m, 59 of 68 river cells ponded).  Spin-up starts from the
   latter.
2. With the deck as shipped, modeled ET is 851 mm against an implied 530 to 780 from P minus
   observed runoff, two to three times the towers in the dormant season.  The Delaware package
   takes it to 795 mm by halving canopy evaporation; transpiration from the inherited canopy
   structure (dormant LAI, year-round SAI 2.0, deciduous phenology on soil temperature) is what
   remains.
3. The converged state with the shipped subsurface drains to a 0.61 m water table and has zero
   summer flow at every outlet; winter peaks are 3 to 8 times observed.  Cause: the CONUS2.1 flow
   barrier is a 0.001 multiplier 7 m down in 1,235 of 1,246 cells, so the aquifer is a 7 m perched
   layer with a transmissivity near 20 m2/day.  The gages recede with e-folding times of 40 to
   137 days, which is a transmissivity of 300 to 1,100 m2/day; the model recedes in 2 to 5 days.
4. Thirteen one-year sensitivity cases from that state: K x0.1 and x10, barrier off, anisotropy
   0.01 and 1.0, roughness raised, the diffusive correction, the full OverlandDiffusive
   condition, horizontal K x100, barrier at 42 m with K x30, and both.  Nothing at the surface
   moves the summer.  Only the barrier at 42 m with horizontal K x100 (vertical as shipped)
   restores baseflow at every outlet.
5. Re-spun on that subsurface: converged in two cycles to a 0.90 m water table with 340 ponded
   cells, perennial flow everywhere, annual flows within 5 to 28 % of the gages, summer about
   half the observed at Wading and Toms, KGE at Pemberton from -1.2 to -0.1, recession 8 to 19
   days.  The Cedar Bridge pine stand is now water-limited in summer (ET bias -0.9 mm/day),
   Silas Little unchanged at +0.6.
