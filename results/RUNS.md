## How to read the names

The prefix is the spin-up family, one per subsurface and domain: pb2 = v1 with the shipped CONUS2.1 subsurface (barrier at 7 m);
pb3 = v1 with the barrier at 42 m and horizontal K x100; pb4 = v2 with the geology-keyed barrier and g4/g5 horizontal K x100;
pb5_k10 and pb5_k20 = v2 re-spun at K x10 and x20.  pb_spin_c1 and pb10_kin are the first two runs, before any package.

The suffix says where a run sits in its family.  `_y1` is the coupled year that starts the sequence; `_rec_cN` is recursion cycle N,
each a full year from the previous cycle's end state, and the last cycle (`_rec_c4`) is the converged state that serves as that family's
control.  Any other suffix is a one-year test from the state named in the start column, changing only what the change column says.
Steady stages (`_seep`, `_overland`) and ten-day solver tests (`solv_*`) are not scored at gages and are not listed.

Read a test against its family's control line, not against other families.  KGE is on daily flow for WY2008 (1 = perfect, 0 = no better
than the mean, negative = worse).  ET bias is model minus tower in mm/day over the tower's days.  Gages are grouped Rancocas (Pemberton,
Rancocas S, Greenwood), Crosswicks, Mullica (Wading WB, Oswego), and coast (Toms, Cedar, Cedar WB, Westecunk).
